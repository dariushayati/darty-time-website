Second screen

One game.
Every screen in the room.

Start a match on the host device and let everyone else follow along over Wi-Fi. No sign-in, no pairing codes to fumble — just the game, live.

  • Host approves every device before it connects
  • Read-only guest view for phones, tablets, and Android TV
  • Game state never leaves your local network

Built for the room

Local-first by design.

No accounts, no cloud

Players, rules, and match history stay on your device. There is no backend to sign into and no data to sync.

Autodarts or touch

Poll a local Autodarts board manager for automatic scoring, or tap the on-screen board with press-and-hold aim. Both are first-class.

Phone, tablet, TV

Phones stay portrait, tablets rotate into a two-pane layout, and Android TV can follow a live game as a read-only guest.

Questions

Before you throw.

What does the beta cost?

Nothing to join. The build is delivered through Google Play's testing track, and you can leave it at any time.

Do I need an Autodarts board?

No. Autodarts and touch scoring are equal first-class paths, so a plain board and a phone are enough.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones in portrait, Android tablets, and Android TV or Google TV as an optional read-only second screen.

Is my data uploaded?

No. Players, settings, and match history stay on your device. Second-screen data stays on your local network. Read the privacy policy.
